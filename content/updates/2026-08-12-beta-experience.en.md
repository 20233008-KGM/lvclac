This update focuses on reducing interruptions in the calculation flow and making the next action easier to find when help is needed.

## More natural first-use guidance

Guidance no longer takes up a separate area that pushes the calculator down. Step numbers now appear beside the relevant inputs, so it is easier to see where each value belongs.

- Guidance follows the trading situation selected during onboarding.
- The help state clears naturally after the calculation is complete.
- It can be opened again from the How to use button at the top.

## More stable calculator resizing

We fixed an issue where the investment risk notice and footer content moved while resizing calculator columns on desktop. The surrounding content now stays in place as the calculation area is adjusted.

## A dedicated contact path

The new contact page shows the official company email and provides actions to copy it or open the user's mail app. Product issues and suggestions can be sent through this path.

This release does not change calculation logic or formulas. It only improves the calculation flow and access to supporting information.
