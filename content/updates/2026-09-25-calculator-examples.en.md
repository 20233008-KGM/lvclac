**Calculation examples** now appear below the calculator. New visitors can select **New here?** to scroll smoothly to them; the shortcut disappears after use. It is hidden for signed-in users and visitors with previous calculator activity.

## Four stages in the actual calculator interface

Choose stock, index, or commodity futures and follow **Initial inputs → Completed inputs → Place an order → Reduce the position**. Highlights identify the fields to enter, while each stage shows how contract counts affect liquidation prices and margin requirements.

The product specification table includes the contract multiplier, margin assumptions, and tick size. The completed calculation also shows the position's profit or loss per tick.

## Read-only examples that keep your calculations intact

The examples use disabled calculator panels. Switching products does not change your live inputs, saved values, or undo history.

Products and trading conditions are fictional teaching examples. Orders are calculator simulations and do not submit real trades.
