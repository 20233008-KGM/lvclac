Switch margin input methods in calculation examples and adjust your memo writing space to fit your needs. We have also made sign-in and registration inputs easier to use.

## Explore examples by margin method

Stock, index, and commodity futures examples now let you choose **rate, amount per contract, or total amount**. The product specifications and input fields update together, making it easier to compare which values to enter.

Follow each method from initial inputs through adding to and reducing a position. Total margin amounts in the examples refer to the starting position.

[View calculation examples](/en#calculator-examples-title)

## Adjust your memo writing space

The memo editor on the records page now provides more writing space. **Drag the handle below the input area up or down** to adjust its height.

You can also focus the handle and use the up and down arrow keys to resize it. Give yourself more room when writing or reviewing a long memo.

## Check the password you entered

Password fields on the sign-in and registration forms now include a **show/hide button**. The password confirmation field on the registration form supports it too.

## Small improvements

- The **How to use** button is available again at the top of the calculator.
- Annual plan savings are now described as a **discount** for clearer pricing information.
