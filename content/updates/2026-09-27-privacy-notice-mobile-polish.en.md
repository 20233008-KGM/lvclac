We have made the privacy notice lighter so it interrupts the calculation flow less. Smaller screens also get a cleaner layout for key tools and guidance text.

## A less disruptive privacy notice

Instead of covering the page with a large prompt on first visit, the consent flow now appears as a compact notice near the bottom of the screen. You can review the calculator while making your choice.

The allow, reject, and settings actions are also easier to tell apart. Button width and spacing have been adjusted so the choices are clearer on both desktop and mobile.

## Mobile layout polish

The calculator header tools now fit more reliably on one row on mobile. Language, help, and primary controls stay aligned more naturally on narrow screens.

We also refined line breaks in the calculation guidance text. Important warnings and explanations should read more comfortably on smaller screens.
